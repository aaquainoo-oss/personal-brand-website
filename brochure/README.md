# The Ultimate Care — Trifold Brochure (Redesign)

Redesign of the TUC trifold, based on the *Brochure Marketing Review and Recommendations*
(September 2026). Keeps the royal-blue and white color scheme, logo, and photography.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Source. 11 × 8.5 in landscape, two sheets (outside + inside). |
| `TUC-Trifold-Brochure.pdf` | Print-ready export (2 pages). |
| `preview-outside.png`, `preview-inside.png` | Quick-look previews. |
| `assets/` | Photos, logo, badges, QR (cropped from the current brochure) and self-hosted fonts. |

To re-export after edits, open `index.html` in Chrome, choose **Print → Save as PDF**,
set margins to **None**, and turn on **Background graphics**.

## Panel plan

| Sheet | Left | Center | Right |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outside | Inside flap: founder story, testimonial, trust marks | Back cover: consultation call to action, QR code, service area, payment | Front cover: benefit headline, services, phone |
| Inside | Services grouped by everyday support, nursing, coordination, with affiliate services marked | Four care levels in plain language | Why TUC: promise and five reasons |

## Confirm before printing

- **Images.** Photos, logo, badges and the QR code were cropped from a photo of the old brochure. Replace them with the original high-resolution files.
- **QR code.** Point it at a consultation-request page, then test the printed code.
- **Testimonial.** Replace the dashed placeholder with an approved, permissioned quote.
- **Care levels.** "FCCM" is written as "family care meeting". Confirm this meaning, and confirm the visit, telehealth and meeting frequency for each level. "Bi-monthly" was read as every two months.
- **Founder bio.** The old brochure said both "19+ years as a registered nurse" and "18+ years of leadership". The redesign uses 19+ years as an RN.
- **Payment.** Medicare (marked "coming soon") and individual insurer names were left out, per the review. Confirm the categories and payer participation.
- **Affiliate services.** Confirm which entity provides rehabilitation, outpatient infusion and medical aesthetics.
- **Service area and awards.** Confirm the counties served, and that each badge is current and authorized for use.
- **Print proof.** Letter-fold panels are equal width (3.667 in). Ask your printer whether the inside flap should be about 1/16 in narrower, and get a color proof.
