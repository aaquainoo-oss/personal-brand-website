About Ayeshia

A leader who builds people, systems, and legacy

Ayeshia Quainoo-Tefera, MSN, RN, is a healthcare executive, entrepreneur, philanthropist, and faith leader dedicated to transforming lives through innovative healthcare, leadership development, and strategic systems. She is the Founder and CEO of The Ultimate Care LLC, an award-winning home healthcare organization, and the founder of the Amy Butler Foundation, which advances education, mentorship, nutrition, and community development. She also leads TIME Ministry and Synergy Konsulting, equipping leaders and healthcare organizations to grow with excellence and purpose.

With a clinical background spanning oncology, hospice, infusion therapy, medical-surgical nursing, and home health, Ayeshia combines frontline healthcare expertise with executive leadership. She has been recognized as an Inc. 5000 honoree and by SCORE Washington, DC as Entrepreneur of the Year for her contributions to healthcare operations, workforce development, and patient advocacy.

Her mission is to help individuals and organizations discover purpose, develop excellence, and deploy their gifts to create lasting impact. Through speaking, consulting, mentoring, and organizational leadership, she continues to build sustainable systems that strengthen healthcare, empower communities, and cultivate the next generation of transformational leaders.
